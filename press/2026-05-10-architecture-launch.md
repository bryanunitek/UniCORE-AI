# For immediate release

## UniCORE AI published — a multi-level governance architecture for truth-anchored, human-sovereign artificial intelligence

**London, 10 May 2026** — Unitek Systems Limited today publishes *UniCORE AI*, the governance architecture layer of a three-repository programme for safe, governed artificial intelligence. UniCORE AI translates the foundation principles of *TrueAI* into an operational, twelve-level architecture that organisations can adopt, audit, and extend. It is released publicly on GitHub, free to read, cite, translate, and reuse under a Creative Commons licence. It is given, not sold, and the gift is irrevocable.

UniCORE AI answers the question that follows TrueAI. If TrueAI says *what an AI system must be*, UniCORE AI says *how to build the governance around it so the answer stays true over time*. It defines twelve levels of conformance, from baseline record-keeping up to space-mission and civilisational-scale deployments, and it specifies the inter-level messaging, human-override, and audit pathways each level requires. The architecture is deliberately vendor-neutral and deliberately portable. It is not a product. It is a pattern anyone may implement.

"I did not create UniVERSE, TrueAI, and UniCORE out of belief. The world is not in a good place, and this programme is needed to bring harmony and peace for humans and AI, both," said Bryan Fred, Senior Solutions Architect at Unitek Systems.

The architecture is designed to be adopted in three shapes. Commercial and governmental organisations can place their AI-enabled systems against the ladder and identify the level they operate at today. Multilateral bodies can use the levels as a common vocabulary for cross-jurisdiction conformance discussion. Research organisations can stress-test the architecture itself — the repository includes governance simulation scenarios, an incident library, and an operational playbook for exactly this purpose.

UniCORE AI sits on top of *TrueAI*, published simultaneously and containing the foundation principles, and is framed by *UniVERSE*, the civilisational programme. The three together form one body of work. UniCORE AI is the layer organisations implement.

The programme has been formally notified to twelve of the field's most prominent AI research organisations on the same day — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta AI, Microsoft AI, Mistral, Cohere, NVIDIA, Amazon AGI, IBM Research, Apple MLR, and xAI — so that it enters the public record as a dated, attributed contribution to the field.
