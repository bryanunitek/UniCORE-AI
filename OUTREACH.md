# OUTREACH

Formal record of outreach made from the UniVERSE / TrueAI / UniCORE-AI
programme to external organisations is maintained centrally in the
**UniVERSE** repository.

The UniCORE-AI repository holds the reference implementation. Outreach
activity is recorded once, at the programme level, in UniVERSE.

## Policy

- UniCORE AI is given, not sold, and irrevocable. The OUTREACH record
  exists to document that the programme was openly offered. No reply is
  required from any recipient.
- Entries in OUTREACH record **the fact of the offer**, not any private
  content a recipient may have sent back.
