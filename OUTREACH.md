## Policy

- UniCORE AI is given, not sold, and irrevocable. The OUTREACH record
  exists to document that the programme was openly offered. No reply is
  required from any recipient.
- Entries in OUTREACH record **the fact of the offer**, not any private
  content a recipient may have sent back.
