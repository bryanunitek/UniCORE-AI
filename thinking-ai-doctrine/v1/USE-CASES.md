# USE-CASES.md — 1H2C practical examples

**Worked use-case examples of the Thinking AI (1H2C): one human, two equal Claws, at AI-Maturity Level 4 — thinking, exploration, modelling, interpretation. Never consequential decisions.**

> Given, not sold. Attribution required. Provided under CC BY 4.0.

Author: Bryan Fred, Unitek Systems Limited, Bedford, United Kingdom.

Each example below is an **AI-Maturity Level 4 (Assistant Institutional AI)** *thinking* use of 1H2C, distinct from the UniCORE AI 12-Level Governance model. In every case the consequential *decision* returns to the human and is taken under **1H1C** at the production tier ([AI-Maturity Level 5 Development Institutional AI / Level 6 Team UniCORE](../../AI-MATURITY-LEVELS.md)).

---

## The principle these examples reinforce

> **A 1H2C is not for consequential decisions. A 1H1C is for consequential decisions. A 1H2C is for thinking.**

The human remains sovereign. The AI does not act, does not decide, and generates no consequence. The two Claws only *think* — and they think to each other **on the TrueAI truth contract** (every claim TRUE / FALSE / UNVERIFIED), human-steered and human-advanced, never self-looping. The canonical role differentiation used below is **Claw 1 = analysis (Proposer-leaning)** and **Claw 2 = construction (Challenger-leaning)**; other differentiations are permitted (see [`FOUNDATION.md`](FOUNDATION.md)).

---

## 1. Medical research — a doctor exploring disease mechanisms

- **Claw 1 (analysis):** reviews literature, models pathways, identifies contradictions.
- **Claw 2 (construction):** proposes experiments, builds structured hypotheses.
- **Human:** chooses direction, validates assumptions, sets ethical boundaries.

**1H2C = thinking.** **1H1C = treatment decisions.**

## 2. Judiciary — a judge interpreting complex case law

- **Claw 1:** extracts relevant cases, highlights conflicts.
- **Claw 2:** constructs interpretations, maps doctrinal branches.
- **Human:** determines what is persuasive, admissible, binding.

**1H2C = thinking.** **1H1C = issuing rulings.**

## 3. Climate science — a researcher modelling extreme weather

- **Claw 1:** analyses historical climate data.
- **Claw 2:** constructs scenario simulations.
- **Human:** selects valid assumptions, interprets results.

**1H2C = thinking.** **1H1C = policy decisions.**

## 4. Finance — a CFO stress-testing a global business

- **Claw 1:** analyses markets, regulatory changes, currency exposure.
- **Claw 2:** constructs stress-test scenarios and mitigation pathways.
- **Human:** sets risk appetite, chooses strategy.

**1H2C = thinking.** **1H1C = financial commitments.**

## 5. Cybersecurity — a director investigating a breach

- **Claw 1:** analyses logs, traces anomalies, identifies attack vectors.
- **Claw 2:** constructs containment strategies and remediation plans.
- **Human:** decides what actions to take.

**1H2C = thinking.** **1H1C = operational response.**

## 6. History and anthropology — a scholar reconstructing the past

- **Claw 1:** analyses archaeological data, linguistic fragments, cultural patterns.
- **Claw 2:** constructs plausible reconstructions and competing theories.
- **Human:** determines credibility and academic direction.

**1H2C = thinking.** **1H1C = publication, academic claims.**

## 7. Corporate strategy — a leader designing a long-range roadmap

- **Claw 1:** analyses markets, competitors, technologies.
- **Claw 2:** constructs scenario maps and strategic pathways.
- **Human:** chooses mission, direction, commitments.

**1H2C = thinking.** **1H1C = corporate decisions.**

## 8. Software architecture — an architect designing a new platform

- **Claw 1:** analyses constraints, dependencies, performance requirements.
- **Claw 2:** constructs architectural patterns and integration models.
- **Human:** selects the final design.

**1H2C = thinking.** **1H1C = implementation decisions.**

## 9. Public policy — an official exploring a regulation

- **Claw 1:** analyses data, precedent, international frameworks.
- **Claw 2:** constructs policy models and impact assessments.
- **Human:** decides what becomes law.

**1H2C = thinking.** **1H1C = legislative action.**

## 10. Education — a teacher designing a curriculum

- **Claw 1:** analyses student needs, learning patterns, subject requirements.
- **Claw 2:** constructs lesson plans, assessments, progression models.
- **Human:** chooses what is taught and how.

**1H2C = thinking.** **1H1C = teaching decisions.**

---

## Summary

A 1H2C is a **thinking engine**, not a decision engine. It is used when the human needs exploration, modelling, hypothesis generation, interpretation, scenario construction, or multi-perspective reasoning. It is **never** used for consequential decisions — that is the role of 1H1C.

Across all ten domains the boundary is the same: **the two Claws think; the human decides.** The moment a consequence attaches, the shape is 1H1C and the human owns the act.
