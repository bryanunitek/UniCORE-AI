# USE-CASES.md — 1H2C practical examples

**Worked use-case examples of the Thinking AI (1H2C): one human, two equal Claws, at AI-Maturity Level 4 — thinking, exploration, modelling, interpretation. Never consequential decisions.**

> Given, not sold. Attribution required. Provided under CC BY 4.0.

Author: Bryan Fred, Unitek Systems Limited, Bedford, United Kingdom.

Each example below is an **AI-Maturity Level 4 (Assistant Institutional AI)** *thinking* use of 1H2C, distinct from the UniCORE AI 12-Level Governance model. In every case the consequential *decision* returns to the human and is taken under **1H1C** at the production tier ([AI-Maturity Level 5 Development Institutional AI / Level 6 Team UniCORE](../../AI-MATURITY-LEVELS.md)).

**How to read these examples — the two Claws THINK TOGETHER.** 1H2C is not two Claws working in separate silos and handing results to a human. It is two equal Claws **bouncing off each other** — one advances a claim, the other challenges and grounds it, the first revises, the second tests again — the way two hemispheres reason to a conclusion neither would reach alone. The thinking happens *in the exchange between them*, always on the TrueAI truth contract (every claim TRUE / FALSE / UNVERIFIED). The human steers the topic, advances each round, and harvests — but the exploration itself is the back-and-forth. Below, **Claw A** leans Proposer and **Claw B** leans Challenger; the arrows (→ ←) mark the bounce. Roles are leanings, not silos: across rounds either Claw may propose or challenge.

## The principle these examples reinforce

> **A 1H2C is not for consequential decisions. A 1H1C is for consequential decisions. A 1H2C is for thinking.**

The human remains sovereign. The AI does not act, does not decide, and generates no consequence. The two Claws only *think* — and they think **to each other**, bouncing back and forth on the **TrueAI truth contract** (every claim TRUE / FALSE / UNVERIFIED), human-steered and human-advanced, never self-looping. The exploration is the exchange itself: one Claw proposes, the other challenges and grounds, the first revises, the second tests — not two silos of parallel work handed up separately. **Claw A** leans Proposer, **Claw B** leans Challenger, but the roles are leanings within a dialogue, not fixed separate jobs (see [`FOUNDATION.md`](FOUNDATION.md)).

## 1. Medical research — a doctor exploring disease mechanisms

The human steers: *"explore why this tumour resists the standard therapy."* The two Claws bounce:

- **A →** proposes a resistance pathway from the literature.
- **B ←** challenges it: two cited studies conflict, and one result is UNVERIFIED — the mechanism can't be asserted as TRUE yet.
- **A →** revises: narrows to the sub-pathway the conflicting studies agree on, proposes an experiment to test it.
- **B ←** stress-tests the experiment design, flags a confound, proposes a control.
- … the exchange continues as the human advances it.

**Human:** steers the direction, validates assumptions, sets ethical boundaries, harvests the grounded hypotheses. **1H2C = thinking together.** **1H1C = treatment decisions.**

## 2. Judiciary — a judge interpreting complex case law

The human steers: *"how do these precedents bear on this fact pattern?"* The two Claws bounce:

- **A →** offers an interpretation and the precedent it rests on.
- **B ←** counters with a conflicting precedent and asks which is more binding.
- **A →** distinguishes the two on their facts; marks one line UNVERIFIED pending the full judgment text.
- **B ←** builds the opposing reading so both live side by side for the judge.
- … back and forth until the doctrinal branches are mapped.

**Human:** determines what is persuasive, admissible, binding. **1H2C = thinking together.** **1H1C = issuing rulings.**

## 3. Climate science — a researcher modelling extreme weather

The human steers: *"model plausible extreme-heat scenarios for this region."* The two Claws bounce:

- **A →** proposes a scenario from historical data.
- **B ←** challenges an assumption as unsupported by the record, marks it UNVERIFIED.
- **A →** re-bases the scenario on the confirmed data, proposes a variant.
- **B ←** runs the variant against a counter-case and reports where they diverge.
- … the models sharpen through the exchange.

**Human:** selects valid assumptions, interprets results. **1H2C = thinking together.** **1H1C = policy decisions.**

## 4. Finance — a CFO stress-testing a global business

The human steers: *"where does this business break under stress?"* The two Claws bounce:

- **A →** proposes a stress scenario (a currency shock plus a regulatory change).
- **B ←** challenges its plausibility, grounds each leg, flags one exposure as UNVERIFIED.
- **A →** revises the scenario and proposes a mitigation pathway.
- **B ←** attacks the mitigation, finds a second-order effect, proposes a hedge.
- … resilience is explored through the argument, not asserted.

**Human:** sets risk appetite, chooses strategy. **1H2C = thinking together.** **1H1C = financial commitments.**

## 5. Cybersecurity — a director investigating a breach

The human steers: *"reconstruct how this breach happened."* The two Claws bounce:

- **A →** proposes an attack vector from the logs.
- **B ←** challenges it: the timeline doesn't fit; one inference is UNVERIFIED without the missing log.
- **A →** proposes an alternative vector consistent with the timeline.
- **B ←** tests it against the artefacts, confirms part, disputes part, proposes what evidence would settle it.
- … the hypothesis is forged in the back-and-forth.

**Human:** decides what actions to take. **1H2C = thinking together.** **1H1C = operational response.**

## 6. History and anthropology — a scholar reconstructing the past

The human steers: *"what plausibly happened at this site?"* The two Claws bounce:

- **A →** proposes a reconstruction from the fragments.
- **B ←** challenges it with a competing reading of the same evidence, marks the weak link UNVERIFIED.
- **A →** defends part, concedes part, proposes a synthesis.
- **B ←** builds the strongest rival theory so both stand for the scholar to judge.
- … competing reconstructions emerge from the exchange.

**Human:** determines credibility and academic direction. **1H2C = thinking together.** **1H1C = publication, academic claims.**

## 7. Corporate strategy — a leader designing a long-range roadmap

The human steers: *"map our credible futures over ten years."* The two Claws bounce:

- **A →** proposes a strategic future and the market read behind it.
- **B ←** challenges the read, flags an assumption as UNVERIFIED, proposes a counter-future.
- **A →** reconciles the two into a branching scenario.
- **B ←** stress-tests each branch against a competitor response.
- … the scenario map is argued into shape.

**Human:** chooses mission, direction, commitments. **1H2C = thinking together.** **1H1C = corporate decisions.**

## 8. Software architecture — an architect designing a new platform

The human steers: *"explore designs for this platform."* The two Claws bounce:

- **A →** proposes an architecture pattern.
- **B ←** challenges it on a constraint (a scaling limit), grounds the concern, proposes a trade-off.
- **A →** revises the pattern to absorb the constraint, proposes an integration model.
- **B ←** tests the integration against a failure mode, reports where it holds and where it doesn't.
- … the options are pressure-tested through dialogue.

**Human:** selects the final design. **1H2C = thinking together.** **1H1C = implementation decisions.**

## 11. Earth-systems monitoring — a standing 1HNC for long-horizon science

This example differs from the ten above: it is a **standing, long-lived** 1HNC that runs continuously (human-advanced, never self-looping), rather than a single exploration.

A researcher wants to explore a contested, long-horizon hypothesis in earth systems — for instance, whether a set of phenomena across very different timescales (geomagnetic drift, solar/auroral activity, jet-stream dynamics, observed extreme weather, wildfires, and natural disasters, against the backdrop of glacial-cycle forcing) are linked, and what they imply. The human steers; the Claws bounce:

- **A →** proposes a linkage or a model connecting two threads.
- **B ←** challenges it hard: these phenomena sit on *causally separate* footings and *vastly different timescales*; it marks any unsupported cross-thread causal claim **UNVERIFIED** or **FALSE**, and tracks the honestly-contested hypotheses as contested.
- **A →** re-grounds on what the evidence actually supports, proposes what data would test the link.
- **B ←** tests against the physical record, confirms what holds, disputes what doesn't.
- … the exchange runs on a standing cadence (a human-owned schedule, on new-data arrival, and on manual steer), building a grounded, truth-marked corpus over time.

**Why 1HNC fits this so well:** a contested long-horizon hypothesis is exactly where ungrounded AI is most dangerous — it will confidently assert a satisfying causal story. The Challenger hemisphere's standing job is to keep every claim honest (TRUE / FALSE / UNVERIFIED), so the human's hypothesis is *rigorously explored and tested* rather than *asserted or dismissed*. Cross-company model composition strengthens this further: agreement across independent vendor substrates is a stronger signal, disagreement surfaces the contested points. The accumulated corpus becomes the researcher's owned thinking model of the domain; its disposition (private / public CC BY 4.0 / mixed) is the human's sovereign call.

**1HNC = thinking together, continuously, on the truth.** **1H1C = any consequential decision, forecast, or public claim** — which returns to the accountable human.

## Summary

A 1H2C is a **thinking engine**, not a decision engine. It is used when the human needs exploration, modelling, hypothesis generation, interpretation, scenario construction, or multi-perspective reasoning. It is **never** used for consequential decisions — that is the role of 1H1C.

Across all ten domains two things hold. First, the two Claws **think together** — the exploration lives in the back-and-forth between them, one proposing, one challenging, both on the truth, not in parallel silos. Second, **the two Claws think; the human decides.** The moment a consequence attaches, the shape is 1H1C and the human owns the act.
