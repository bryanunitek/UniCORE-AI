## Why this file exists

The TrueAI [Singular Pairing Principle §8](https://git.unitek-systems.com/UniCORE/TrueAI/src/branch/main/docs/10001-Singular-Pairing-Principle.md) (mirror: [GitHub](https://github.com/bryanunitek/TrueAI/blob/main/docs/10001-Singular-Pairing-Principle.md)) permits variants that depart from the default 1H1C shape, under CC BY 4.0, and requires three things of any such variant:

1. A variant is **untested theory** until independently demonstrated across multiple Solutions in regulated settings.
2. A variant **cannot claim** `Powered by UniCORE AI` / built-on-the-TrueAI-Foundation **certification** — the certification gate recognises 1H1C only.
3. A variant **must declare itself** as a variant; hiding it inside a TrueAI claim is itself a breach of Invariant 8 (Transparency Without Exception).

1H2C is such a variant (two Claws rather than one). This file is its declaration, stated in the truth contract's own terms.

## What is TRUE

- **TRUE:** 1H2C is a declared departure from the default Singular Pairing shape (1H1C), permitted for experimentation under CC BY 4.0 by Singular Pairing Principle §8.
- **TRUE:** 1H2C is scoped to AI-Maturity Level 4 (Assistant Institutional AI) thinking/exploration only, and is excluded by its own doctrine from consequential decisions and from the AI-Maturity Level 5 (Development Institutional AI) / Level 6 (Team UniCORE) production tier. ("Level 4" here is the six-level AI-Maturity Framework, distinct from the UniCORE AI 12-Level Governance model.)
- **TRUE:** 1H2C is constructed to satisfy all Nine Invariants, with explicit conformance for Invariants 1, 3, and 9 (see [`INVARIANT-CONFORMANCE.md`](INVARIANT-CONFORMANCE.md)).
- **TRUE:** the inter-Claw channel is mandatorily on the TrueAI truth contract; this is what fixes 1H2C at AI-Maturity Level 4.
- **TRUE:** the two-hemisphere principle — that friction between two equal, differentiated reasoners can surface what one reasoner asserts unexamined — is a coherent, well-motivated hypothesis grounded in the analogy to human hemispheric cognition.

## What is UNVERIFIED

- **UNVERIFIED:** that 1H2C actually produces *better thinking* than a single careful Claw. The hypothesis is well-motivated but has **not** been demonstrated with evidence. Analogy is not proof. Per §8, it is untested theory until independently demonstrated.
- **UNVERIFIED:** that the specific Proposer / Challenger differentiation is the optimal or even a sufficient hemisphere split. It is the canonical proposal, not a proven configuration.
- **UNVERIFIED:** the operational cost/benefit of two Claws versus one for any given task.

## What is FALSE (must never be claimed)

- **FALSE:** that 1H2C is certified, or may wear the `Powered by UniCORE AI` mark, or the maturity shield. It may not. The certification gate recognises 1H1C only.
- **FALSE:** that 1H2C is a replacement for, or an upgrade to, the Singular Pairing Principle. It is a scoped variant for non-consequential thinking; the Singular Pairing Principle remains the recommended and only certifiable topology for consequential production.
- **FALSE:** that 1H2C relaxes any Nine-Invariant constraint. It varies the pairing shape only; the Foundation is not varied.

## What 1H2C may and may not claim

**May claim:**

- "A TrueAI-aligned thinking-AI *variant*, declared under Singular Pairing Principle §8, scoped to AI-Maturity Level 4 (Assistant Institutional AI) exploration."
- "Constructed to satisfy the Nine Invariants (see conformance record)."
- "Untested theory — experimental."

**May not claim:**

- `Powered by UniCORE AI`, `TrueAI-certified`, `UniCORE-certified`, or the maturity shield.
- Any implication that its benefit over 1H1C is demonstrated.
- Any use in consequential, regulated, or third-party-defensible decision-making.

## Mandatory disclosure in use

Any deployment or demonstration of 1H2C that claims *any* form of TrueAI alignment — even non-certified — must state, visibly, that it is a 1H2C variant under Singular Pairing Principle §8, untested, non-certifiable, and scoped to AI-Maturity Level 4 (Assistant Institutional AI). Presenting 1H2C output as if produced under the certifiable default, or omitting the variant disclosure, is an Invariant 8 breach.
