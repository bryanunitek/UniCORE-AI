## Purpose — public disclosure of the design space

This document publicly discloses, with a date and attribution, the **design space of multi-Claw thinking** — every axis along which a Thinking AI (one human, N Claws) can be configured, and the configurations those axes generate. It is a **defensive publication**: by placing the design space on the public record under CC BY 4.0, the configurations described here become **prior art**, so they cannot be claimed as novel or patented by others.

**Honest scope of that claim.** Public disclosure makes the *disclosed design space* prior art and blocks novel patents over these configurations in most jurisdictions. It does not, and cannot, guarantee that no genuinely novel implementation *mechanism* outside this disclosure could ever be patented. What this document establishes is that **the configurations of multi-Claw thinking — the shapes themselves — are publicly owned and cannot be claimed as new.**

**This list is illustrative, not limiting.** The design space is open-ended. The axes below generate far more combinations than can be individually named, and new combinations along these axes are part of the *same disclosed space*, not exceptions to it. Nothing in this enumeration should be read as excluding, by omission, any configuration that combines the disclosed axes. The disclosure is of the **space and its generating axes**, of which the named examples are instances — **it is not limited to them.**

## The generating axes

Every multi-Claw thinking configuration is a point in a space defined by four independent axes. Any combination of values across these axes is a valid configuration and is disclosed here as part of the space.

### Axis 1 — N: the number of Claws

Two or more, bounded by the human's integrative capacity:

- **1H2C** — one human, two Claws. The minimum viable case (the fewest that create friction rather than echo). The memorable headline form.
- **1H3C** — three Claws (e.g. proposer / challenger / synthesiser).
- **1H4C** — four Claws (e.g. proposer / two challengers / synthesiser; or proposer / challenger / devil's advocate / synthesiser).
- **1H5C … 1HNC** — any N ≥ 2, up to the ceiling where one human can still genuinely steer, advance, integrate, and own every round. Past that ceiling it is no longer 1HNC (it becomes the emergent swarm the Foundation forbids — see [`INVARIANT-CONFORMANCE.md`](INVARIANT-CONFORMANCE.md)).

**1H2C is liked and used as the headline; 1HNC is the understood general truth.** Both name the same doctrine at different resolutions.

### Axis 2 — model composition

- **All-same-model** — every Claw runs on the same AI model. Simplest and cheapest; differentiation must come from *role* or the Claws echo one another.
- **All-different-models** — every Claw runs on a different model. Maximum substrate diversity; different training, different blind spots, strongest friction.
- **Partial mix** — any blend (e.g. two Claws on model A, one on model B). Every blend across the N Claws is a valid configuration in the space.

### Axis 3 — provider / vendor composition

- **Single-provider** — all Claws from one AI company (OpenClaw, or any single equivalent).
- **Cross-provider / cross-company** — Claws drawn from *different* AI companies (for example, one on each of several vendors' models, and/or open-weights models). This yields a distinct and valuable property (see "Cross-company consensus" below).
- **Provider-agnostic throughout** — the doctrine names no required vendor. OpenClaw or any equivalent TrueAI-aligned Claw. This is itself part of why the pattern cannot be owned by one vendor: it is vendor-independent by construction — given, not sold.

### Axis 4 — role composition

- **Proposer / Challenger** (the minimum dialectic — the 1H2C canonical form).
- **Proposer / Challenger / Synthesiser.**
- **Proposer / multiple Challengers** (several independent lines of doubt).
- **Devil's advocate** as a dedicated role.
- **Record-holder + reasoner(s)** (one Claw holds the grounded corpus, others reason against it).
- **Human-framing side + grounded-record side** (reconciling the gap between how the human frames a question and what the grounded record supports).
- Any other role assignment the human configures, provided the Claws remain **equal in authority** (none commands another) and the exchange stays **on TrueAI**.

## Cross-company consensus — a first-class, valuable variant

A configuration worth naming explicitly: **Claws drawn from different AI companies, reasoning to a consensus across vendors.**

When Claws built on different companies' models (and/or open-weights models) bounce off each other on the TrueAI truth contract, a claim that survives challenge across *independent vendor substrates* is grounded more strongly than a claim from any single vendor. No one company's training bias dominates; agreement across companies is a stronger signal of TRUE, and disagreement surfaces exactly where a single-vendor answer would have hidden an unexamined assumption. Cross-company consensus turns vendor diversity into a truth-strengthening mechanism.

This is disclosed here as a named configuration in the open design space. Like every configuration in this document, it is illustrative of the space, not a limit on it.

## The space is a matrix (illustrative, not limiting)

The four axes multiply. A single configuration is chosen by picking a value on each axis, for example:

| Example configuration | N | Models | Providers | Roles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum case | 2 | same | single | proposer / challenger |
| Rich single-vendor | 3 | same | single | proposer / challenger / synthesiser |
| Diverse-substrate | 3 | different | cross-company | proposer / challenger / synthesiser |
| Cross-company consensus | 4 | different | cross-company (4 vendors) | proposer / 2 challengers / synthesiser |
| Record-grounded | 3 | mixed | cross-company | record-holder / reasoner / challenger |

These rows are **examples of points in the space**, not the whole space and not a limit on it. Any valid combination of the four axes — including combinations not tabulated here — is part of the same disclosed design space.

## What holds across every variant

No matter which point in the space is chosen, every configuration is bound by the same invariants (see [`FOUNDATION.md`](FOUNDATION.md) and [`INVARIANT-CONFORMANCE.md`](INVARIANT-CONFORMANCE.md)):

- **Always on TrueAI** — every inter-Claw claim is TRUE / FALSE / UNVERIFIED; no Claw lies to another.
- **Human-steered and human-advanced** — no self-perpetuating loop; the human advances every round.
- **N-bounded** — N never exceeds the human's capacity to steer, integrate, and own; beyond that ceiling it is not 1HNC.
- **Human-owned** — one human owns the thinking and the accumulated thinking model; an AI is never accountable.
- **Thinking, not deciding** — AI-Maturity Level 4; consequential decisions return to the human under 1H1C.

These invariants are the boundary of the space. Everything inside them is disclosed here as part of the public, dated, CC BY 4.0 record — and the disclosure is of the space itself, **not limited to the examples named.**
