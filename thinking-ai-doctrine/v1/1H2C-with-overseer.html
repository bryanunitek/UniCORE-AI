The Thinking AI, running — one human, two thinking AIs, one overseer

The human stays at the centre and owns every conclusion. Two equal AIs think against each other on TrueAI (one proposes, one challenges). A third AI oversees the pair — keeping them honest, on-topic and in budget — so one person can run several thinking teams at once without ever handing over the wheel.
