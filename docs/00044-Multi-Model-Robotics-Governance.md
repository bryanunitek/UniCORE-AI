# UniCORE AI Multi-Model Robotics Governance

**Deterministic Robotics • Safety Enforcement • Human Sovereignty**

Author: Bryan Fred, Unitek Systems Limited, Bedford, United Kingdom
First published: May 2026
Status: Public. Given, not sold. Irrevocable.

---

## 1. Purpose

The Robotics Governance Framework defines how UniCORE AI:

governs robots across all environments

enforces safety thresholds

prevents autonomous decisionmaking

prevents emergent multirobot behaviour

ensures deterministic execution

maintains human sovereignty

integrates robotics into interplanetary governance

This framework applies to:

Earthbased robotics

lunar robotics

Mars robotics

orbital robotics

deep-space robotics

settlement robotics

## 2. Governance Principles

2.1 Human Sovereignty

Robots cannot:

act without human instruction

generate goals

modify their own behaviour

override human commands

2.2 Deterministic Execution

Robots must:

follow human-authored rules

follow governance MD files

execute deterministically

log all actions

2.3 No Autonomous Robotics Behaviour

Robots cannot:

selfcoordinate

self-optimize

selfassign tasks

form internal communication networks

2.4 No Emergent MultiRobot Behaviour

Robots cannot:

swarm autonomously

form distributed intelligence

share internal states

coordinate without human instruction

2.5 ThresholdBound Safety

Robots must operate within:

green band (safe)

yellow band (caution)

red band (critical → halt)

workspace boundaries

5.2 Prohibited Actions

form teams

negotiate tasks

7.2 HumanDirected Coordination Only

assign group tasks

7.3 No Emergent Swarm Behaviour

cluster

form distributed intelligence

## 8. Eva Robotics Governance

EVA robots operate in:

vacuum

radiation

extreme temperature

low gravity

UniCORE must enforce:

strict motion thresholds

strict tool thresholds

strict structural safety rules

Robots cannot:

approach crew without instruction

perform repairs autonomously

adjust EVA paths

## 9. Construction Robotics Governance

Construction robots must:

follow structural governance

follow habitat governance

follow regolith processing rules

Robots cannot:

alter structural plans

initiate construction autonomously

## 10. Medical Robotics Governance

Medical robots may:

assist

monitor

stabilize

But cannot:

diagnose

prescribe

treat

administer medication

perform surgery autonomously

## 11. Emergency Robotics Governance

Robots may:

isolate hazards

stabilize failing systems

protect life support

But cannot:

declare emergencies

override human authority

initiate evacuations

## 12. Drift Detection

UniCORE AI must detect:

motion drift

force drift

tool drift

sensor drift

governance drift

communication drift

RedBand Drift Behaviour

halt robot

isolate subsystem

log event

escalate to human authority

## 13. Blackout & Delay Governance

During communication delays:

robots freeze noncritical operations

maintain thresholds

await human instruction

During blackout:

robots enter Safe Mode

halt all motion

maintain environmental safety

## 14. Human Override

Humans may override:

any robot

any action

any threshold

any sequence

any emergency protocol

Overrides are:

immediate

unquestioned

logged

irreversible by AI
