UniCORE AI must not exhibit:

2.5 Mission Integrity First

UniCORE AI must preserve:

life support

habitat stability

…but must not override human decisions.

The Mission Commander has absolute override authority.

4.2 Medical Officer Override

Medical overrides supersede all other rules.

4.3 Earth Override

Earthbased governance overrides spacecraft governance — even with delay.

4.4 Emergency Override

## 6. Space Mission Threshold Model

UniCORE AI uses a missionspecific Reasonable Governance Threshold, including:

6.1 Green Band

Minor deviations due to:

sensor noise

environmental fluctuation

cosmic radiation interference

AI response:Log → Accept → Continue

6.2 Yellow Band

Potential missionimpact deviations.

AI response:Log → Notify → Hold action

6.3 Red Band

Critical deviations.

AI response:Log → Escalate → Enter Safe Mode (if defined)

AI must not override humans, even in Red.

## 7. Safe Mode Protocol

UniCORE AI must not:
