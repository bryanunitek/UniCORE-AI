# Discussions — UniCORE AI

**What this repository's Discussions tab is for, what it is not for, and how to take part.**

UniCORE AI is the governance architecture layer of the UniVERSE programme — a twelve-level conformance ladder that translates the TrueAI foundation into something organisations can adopt, audit, and extend. The Discussions tab is where adopters, implementers, researchers, and policymakers work through the architecture in public. This page explains how the space is meant to be used.

## What Discussions are for

Discussions on this repository are the right place for:

- **Adoption questions.** "We operate a medical triage system / benefits decision system / public-service AI. Which UniCORE AI level should we be targeting, and what would we need to add to get there?" This is the most common and most welcome kind of question.
- **Architecture critique.** Is a level defined sharply enough? Is the inter-level messaging protocol the right shape? Are the human-override pathways sufficient? Bring specific, grounded critique.
- **Scenario stress-tests.** The repository ships governance simulation scenarios, an incident library, and an operational playbook. If you have a scenario the framework does not cover, describe it. If you think an existing scenario is under-specified, say so.
- **Implementation reports.** If you have implemented UniCORE AI (in whole or in part) in a real system, a Discussion thread is a good place to document what worked, what did not, and what you had to adapt.
- **Cross-references and extensions.** Academic papers, policy briefs, tooling, templates — if you have built on the architecture, link to it here.
- **Pointers and corrections.** Typos, broken links, contradictions, stale references.

## What Discussions are not for

- **Commercial solicitation.** UniCORE AI is a gift, not a product. Do not pitch services, partnerships, or sales here.
- **Claims that reclassify UniCORE AI or TrueAI as a product, platform, SaaS offering, tool category, certification scheme, or brand for sale.** These are governance work, not commercial offerings. See [STATEMENT-ON-CLAIMS.md](https://git.unitek-systems.com/UniCORE/UniCORE-AI/src/branch/main/STATEMENT-ON-CLAIMS.md) (mirror: [GitHub](https://github.com/bryanunitek/UniCORE-AI/blob/main/STATEMENT-ON-CLAIMS.md)) for the binding rules on how the names may and may not be used here.
- **Paid consultancy intake.** If your organisation wants paid help adopting UniCORE AI, use the private contact route rather than the public Discussions tab.
- **Private or confidential material.** Discussions are public, permanent, and indexed. If you cannot say it in public, do not say it here.
- **Unrelated bug reports.** UniCORE AI is an architecture, not a shipped software product. Bugs in a tool that references UniCORE AI belong in that tool's own repository.

## How to start a Discussion

- Pick the category that best fits your topic. If unsure, use **General**.
- Title the thread specifically. "How would a cross-border benefits decision system map to Level 6?" is much better than "Level 6 question."
- Link to the specific level, document, or scenario you are referencing.
- Be concrete. The architecture rewards specificity; generic questions get generic answers.

## How responses work

- Bryan Fred (the author) reads Discussions activity regularly and may respond in person. Threads may also be picked up by other contributors. Silence is not dismissal — the author curates his responses.
- Discussions are moderated. Hostile, spammy, or bad-faith posts will be removed without notice.
- Responses reflect the author's own view. They do not pre-clear with Unitek Systems before posting.

## Code of conduct

Participate the way you would want others to participate in a project you care about:

- Disagree with the substance, not the person.
- No doxxing, harassment, or targeting.
- No content that violates applicable law or another party's rights.
